HouseBills keeps track of your household bills.

WHAT THIS SETUP INSTALLS
- The HouseBills application, for your Windows user only. Nothing else is needed: no separate .NET or database installation, and no administrator rights.

YOUR DATA
- Your bills are stored in a single file on this PC: %LOCALAPPDATA%\HouseBills\HouseBills.db. It is created automatically the first time you start the app.
- To back up your data or move it to another PC, copy that file while HouseBills is closed.
- Uninstalling HouseBills keeps your data, so reinstalling brings your bills back.
